How to label this call
  1. Listen to the whole call. Read along in the transcript.
  2. When the caller uses a scam tactic, select those words and pick the tactic. Click a word to select it, shift-click to extend, or drag across words.
  3. Mark every personal detail you hear: names, phone and card numbers, codes, addresses, emails. Trust your ears over the transcript. If the words are wrong or missing, press [ and ] while it plays to select by time.
  4. Press Finish when you have been through the whole call.

You will not see what Baitreel found. That is on purpose: your labels are the answer key it is scored against. Everything is saved as you go.
